# Field Salesperson Guide

## Work a planned visit

1. Open **Field Sales → My Work → Today's Visits** or **My Daily Plans**.
2. Open the customer visit and confirm the customer and planned date.
3. Select **Check In**. Allow browser location access when prompted. The server records the authoritative timestamp and assesses the submitted coordinates against company policy.
4. If location is unavailable, inaccurate, outside the configured radius, or under review, read the server result. Do not retry by changing the reported values.
5. Select **Check Out** when the meeting ends. Checkout records the server time and duration; the visit still needs a business outcome.
6. Enter the outcome, meeting summary, feedback or objection, product interests, and any required follow-up. Select **Complete Visit**.

## Follow-ups and missed visits

Use **My Follow-ups** for assigned Odoo activities. Due dates and assigned users are managed under normal activity permissions. Mark a visit missed only when it was not executed, and provide the required reason. A missed visit is not a completed meeting and has no fabricated GPS evidence.

## GPS requirements

The browser needs location permission and a secure context. Device location may be unavailable or inaccurate and can be spoofed; the application does not provide cryptographic location attestation or physical-device GPS certification.
