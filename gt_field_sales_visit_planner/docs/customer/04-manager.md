# Sales Manager Guide

## Plan a day

Create a daily plan for an eligible salesperson, territory, company, and local visit date. Add authorized customers, order the stops, review the sequence, then confirm the plan. Use the reschedule or cancellation workflow to preserve visit history; do not silently edit execution evidence.

## Monitor execution

Use the dashboard and reports to compare planned, completed, missed, and unexecuted visits. Coverage represents distinct allocated customers with completed visits during the selected period; it is not revenue or GPS proof. Follow-up lists reflect standard Odoo activities. GPS exception status is separate from business completion.

## Review exceptions

Open an authorized location exception, inspect its classification and explanation, and approve or reject it with a reason. The review does not change the original GPS classification. Users may not review their own submitted exception.

## Exports

Use report exports only for authorized business purposes. Routine summary exports omit precise GPS coordinates. Apply the same company and date scope used for the report.
